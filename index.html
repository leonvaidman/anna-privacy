Privacy Policy

Anna, the AI assistant to Leon Vaidman, operated by SandboxLabs.
Effective 9 November 2026.

Anna is an AI assistant that works for Leon Vaidman. She helps arrange meetings, confirm appointments and coordinate with people on his behalf over WhatsApp, Telegram and email. This policy explains what information Anna handles when you communicate with her, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Anna is an AI system, not a person. She tells you so the first time she writes to you.

Who is responsible

SandboxLabs operates Anna and is responsible for the information described here. Contact: leonvaidman@gmail.com.

What information Anna handles

InformationWhere it comes from
Your phone number or messaging account ID, and the profile name you have set WhatsApp or Telegram, when you message Anna

Anna does not ask for and does not need payment details, government ID numbers or passwords. Please do not send them.

Why it is used

  • To read and answer your messages on Leon's behalf.
  • To schedule, confirm, move or follow up on meetings with Leon.
  • To keep the context of an ongoing conversation, so you don't have to repeat yourself.
  • To keep a record of what Anna did and why, so Leon can review it and correct mistakes.

Leon reviews and approves anything substantive before Anna sends it. Anna does not make commitments about price, terms, money or legal matters.

Your information is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used by SandboxLabs to train AI models.

Who processes it for us

Anna relies on these service providers, which process information on our behalf and under their own terms:

  • Meta (WhatsApp) and Telegram: deliver messages between you and Anna.
  • Anthropic: provides the AI model that reads messages and drafts replies.
  • Google: hosts Anna's service and data (Google Cloud), and provides email and calendar.

We share your information with no one else, except where the law requires it.

How long it is kept

  • Voice recordings are deleted once they have been transcribed.
  • Message transcripts and the record of Anna's actions are kept for 3 years, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

You can stop messaging Anna at any time. You can also ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you,
  • correct it, or
  • delete it.

To request deletion, email leonvaidman@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the phone number, Telegram account or email address you used. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days. We may keep a minimal record that a deletion took place.

Security

Access to Anna's data is limited to Leon and the systems that run Anna. Keys and passwords are kept in a dedicated secrets store, separate from the data. Telegram chats with bots are not end-to-end encrypted, so please don't send highly confidential material to Anna there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
